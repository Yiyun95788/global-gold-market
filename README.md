# Global Gold Market Project
This project is the final project for the course CSC316: Data Visualization: Foundations and Advanced Programming at the University of Toronto.

## Overview
### Project Description
The Global Gold Market Project explores the drivers of the current gold market boom by creating various insightful visualizations. The project integrated multiple datasets from reliable sources, and together producing a compelling data story that not only showcases gold’s current record highs, but also explains why this moment is unusual in historical context.\
Steps and decisions of this project are recorded in our [process book](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_T0myRcBCHLo46875zb36hIC_gwhNh9MgXAIH36EucY/edit?usp=sharing).

## Data Sources & Citations
### Market News & Academic Research
- **Reuters (2025).** *Gold blazes past $3,800 in record run, silver nears fresh peak.*
Recent market report highlighting extreme price movements in gold and silver.
https://www.reuters.com/world/india/gold-blazes-past-3800-record-run-silver-nears-fresh-peak-2025-10-01/
- **Bouri, E. (2024).** *Gold as a hedge and safe haven: A time-varying perspective.* **Finance Research Letters, 66, 104187.**
Academic analysis of gold’s dynamic hedge/safe-haven properties.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2024.104187

### Gold Price Data
- **XAU/USD Historical Data (2004–2025)** – Long-term daily gold prices for trend, return, and volatility analysis.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/novandraanugrah/xauusd-gold-price-historical-data-2004-2024
- **TradingEconomics Gold Data** – Real-time and historical gold prices, charts, and market news.
https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold
- **Gold Futures Prices (2009–2024)** – Futures data to analyze spot–futures divergence and unusual market events.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gvyshnya/gold-future-prices

### Cross-Asset Comparisons
- **Gold / BTC / Oil Dataset** – Includes Bitcoin, gold, oil, and S&P 500 prices for cross-asset volatility and store-of-value analysis.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prasertk/bitcon-gold-oil-sp-500/data
- **Gold Reserves by Country (Quarterly)** – Central bank gold holdings for geopolitical and macroeconomic comparison.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/eliasdabbas/gold-reserves-by-country-quarterly

### Currency Data
- **Gold in Other Currencies (Goldhub)** – Gold denominated in various global currencies.
https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data/gold-prices

### Long-Term Historical Data
- **MeasuringWorth Gold/Silver Data (1687–Present)** – Multi-century gold prices, gold/silver ratios, and UK/US market histories.
https://www.measuringworth.com/datasets/gold/result.php

### Macroeconomic Indicators
- **Global Inflation Data** – Inflation indicators to study gold as an inflation hedge.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sazidthe1/global-inflation-data
